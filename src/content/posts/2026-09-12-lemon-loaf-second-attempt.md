The first loaf came out heavy and I told everyone it was meant to be like that. This one I actually meant to be like that, which is a different feeling, even though the loaf itself is more or less identical.

I zested three lemons and then stood there smelling my hands for a while. That's the part nobody puts in the recipe. Zest, then stand in the kitchen with your hands up like a surgeon, for no reason anyone can explain.

![The loaf on a plate, or at least a drawing of the idea of one](/src/assets/uploads/placeholder-plate.jpg)

The glaze went on too early. It slid off the sides and pooled on the plate, and then I ate the pool with a spoon, and I want to be clear that this was the best part.

It is quite dense. The crumb is tight, the way a pound cake is tight, and I have decided to describe this as "sturdy" whenever anyone asks. Nobody has asked.

Next time: less glaze, or more patience, whichever arrives first. The lemons were good. The lemons are always good; it's the rest of us that need work.
