I fed the starter on Tuesday. I fed it again on Wednesday. On Thursday it looked at me the way a cat looks at you when you've bought the wrong food, and I should have taken the hint.

I made the dough anyway. Mixed it, folded it, let it sit under the tea towel with the good pattern on it, the one I use when I want things to go well. It sat there for nine hours doing, as far as I can tell, nothing at all.

## What I think went wrong

I have a list. I always have a list, afterwards.

- the kitchen was cold, and I was too stubborn to move the bowl
- the starter was hungry, not lively, and there is a difference I keep forgetting
- I opened the oven door to look, twice, which is one more time than "never"

There's a line in the book I learned from that I keep coming back to, mostly because it's kinder than I am:

> Bread is patient with you. It is not, however, infinitely patient.

So it came out flat. About the height of a paperback lying down. Dense in the middle, crust like a good idea, and it made honestly decent toast, which is what I told everyone, and which was true.

I've started a new starter. It's called Second. I'm not attached to it. I say that out loud near the jar, so it knows.
