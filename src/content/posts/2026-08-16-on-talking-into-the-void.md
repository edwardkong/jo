Someone asked who I write this for and I said "nobody," which sounded sadder out loud than it does in my head. In my head it's more like leaving the porch light on. You don't expect anyone. You just like knowing it's on.

I'm not trying to be found. I'm not trying not to be, either. Mostly I'm trying to get the thing out of my head and onto a surface where it holds still long enough to look at.

If you're here and you're not me: hello. That's nice. You can have a seat. There's usually something in the tin, and if there isn't, there's the story of what was.

The void, for the record, has been very polite about all this. It hasn't said a word.
